2 Weeks in Southeast Asia

A perfect introduction to Thailand and Vietnam.

Overview

This itinerary is built for those who want the vibrant culture, street food, and stunning temples of Southeast Asia, but also value a quiet room, air conditioning, and a comfortable bed at night. It's paced to prevent burnout while ensuring you see the highlights.

Who This Trip Is For

Couples or individuals wanting cultural immersion without backpacker hostels. Budget conscious but not budget constrained.

What We'd Do Differently

We'd spend an extra night in Chiang Mai to take a dedicated cooking class instead of packing it into a temple day.

The Route

Days 1-3: Bangkok, Thailand

Settle in. Explore the Grand Palace and Wat Pho in the morning before the heat hits. Evenings are for street food tours in Chinatown.

Days 4-7: Chiang Mai, Thailand

Fly north. Visit the ethical Elephant Nature Park, explore the Sunday Night Market, and enjoy the calmer, cooler vibe of the mountains.

Days 8-11: Hanoi, Vietnam

Fly to Hanoi. Wander the Old Quarter, drink egg coffee, and take a food walking tour to understand the local cuisine.

Days 12-14: Ninh Binh, Vietnam

Take a luxury transfer to the "Halong Bay on Land." Take a peaceful boat ride through the limestone karsts before flying home.

Where We Stayed

We prioritize comfortable, boutique, mid-range spots (averaging $60-$120/night) that offer privacy and excellent service.

  • Bangkok: Riva Surya (River views, away from the chaos)
  • Chiang Mai: Tamarind Village (Oasis in the old city)
  • Hanoi: La Siesta Classic (Exceptional service)
  • Ninh Binh: Tam Coc Garden Resort (Boutique nature escape)
